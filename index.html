      How to play

      ←→Switch lanes with the arrows, A/D or 1–4. On touch, swipe, tap a side, or tap an answer chip.
      ✦The right door hides a gem. A wrong door drops a rock and cuts your speed.
      🔥Fever: five correct in a row makes points ×3 until you miss.
      👑Boss gate every 5th question: answers reveal one by one and you get less time. A right answer pays ×5 points. A wrong one drops a boulder that cuts your speed to 40% and smashes through shields.
      🏮Lantern: run through it to burn away one wrong curtain.
      🛡Shield blocks one rock. ⏳ Slow-mo gives you more time to think. 2× doubles points for 20 s.
      ✓✗True/False gates have green and red curtains. “Any correct” gates have more than one right door.
